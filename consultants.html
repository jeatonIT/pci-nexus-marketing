How It Works

From onboarding to audit-ready - for every client.

Add clients, assign SAQ types, and let PCI-Nexus do the heavy lifting. Your clients get guided compliance workflows. You get full visibility and control.

1

Create your consultant account

Sign up for a consultant plan. You get a centralized dashboard showing all your clients' compliance posture at a glance.

2

Add client organizations

Invite clients by email or create their tenant directly. Set access levels - full access, read-only, or assessment-only.

3

Assign SAQ types & scope

Walk each client through the SAQ Wizard. PCI-Nexus loads the correct requirements and evidence templates automatically.

4

Track & remediate findings

Use the Findings & Remediation Kanban to assign tasks, track progress, and generate gap analysis reports for each client.

5

Generate QSA-ready packages

Export a complete evidence package, gap analysis, and compliance report for each client - formatted for QSA submission.

6

Bill clients your way

Choose per-client billing (tenant pays directly) or aggregate billing (you pay, you charge). Flexible for any engagement model.
